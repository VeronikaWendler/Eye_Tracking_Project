Study1 — ES / EE / SP slope comparison
==================================================================================

WHAT THE SLOPE MEANS
For each modality, slope summarizes how strongly the inferred/explicit subjective E-option values track objective E-option p(win).
Slope ≈ 1: close to unbiased value sensitivity.
Slope ≈ 0: strong compression / little tracking of objective E value.
