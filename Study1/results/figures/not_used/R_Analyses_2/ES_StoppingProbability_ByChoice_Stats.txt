WITHIN-ES STOPPING PROBABILITY BY EVENTUAL CHOICE
================================================

E = eventual experiential choice
S = eventual symbolic choice

IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION:
This analysis is conditional on eventual choice.  It compares E-ending and S-ending sampling trajectories;  it does not establish that stopping causes the final choice.
